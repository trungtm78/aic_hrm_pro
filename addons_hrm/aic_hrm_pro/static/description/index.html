AIC HRM Pro — OKR & KPI that read like an audited record

One engine for objectives, weighted KPIs, check-ins, monitoring and performance reviews — built for enterprises that manage by objectives, not spreadsheets.

The full PDCA loop, not a data store

  • Plan — objectives with weights, committed vs aspirational, cross-cycle alignment (quarter goals cascade into annual ones), approval workflow, KPI catalog with the full documentation form: direction, aggregation (final / average / cumulative), source, thresholds.
  • Do — weekly check-ins with confidence and blockers; values write straight through to scores.
  • Check — configurable alert rules (stale goals, red streaks, falling confidence) with manager escalation, review meetings with auto-built agendas, a leadership cockpit and an alignment tree.
  • Act — mid-cycle target changes only through approved, fully-audited revisions; review cycles with anonymous 360 feedback, calibration with mandatory justifications, 9-box, development and improvement plans.

Made for management, not just tracking

  • Personal scorecards enforce weights summing to exactly 100%.
  • Scores roll up on the Google 0.0–1.0 scale with configurable red/amber/green thresholds; status always pairs color with shape.
  • Locked cycles are immutable; every correction leaves a paper trail.
  • 360 feedback is anonymous by construction — no chatter trail, no author metadata, aggregates only above a minimum rater count.
