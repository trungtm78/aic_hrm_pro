# OKR/KPI Library — Research Sources

The seed data in `aic_hrm_library/data/` was written for this product with
its own wording. The numbers are editable industry reference points, not
promises. The following public references informed the choice of metrics
and typical target ranges:

## OKR methodology and examples

- What Matters (John Doerr) — OKR examples by function: https://www.whatmatters.com/get-examples
- Google re:Work — OKR guide and 0.0–1.0 grading: https://rework.withgoogle.com/en/guides/set-goals-with-okrs
- Profit.co — OKR examples by department: https://www.profit.co/blog/okr-university/
- Perdoo — OKR vs KPI distinction: https://www.perdoo.com/resources/

## KPI definitions and benchmarks

- KPI Institute / kpi.org — KPI documentation standards: https://www.kpi.org/
- Klipfolio — KPI examples by team: https://www.klipfolio.com/resources/kpi-examples
- DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) — engineering delivery metrics
  (deployment frequency, change failure rate, MTTR, lead time):
  https://dora.dev/
- ISO 30414 — human capital reporting metric groups (wording is
  "inspired by", not copied)

## Industry-specific references

- OEE Industry Standard (~85% world-class) — https://www.oee.com/
- APICS / ASCM — inventory turnover, stockout, GMROI concepts:
  https://www.ascm.org/
- SPI Research — professional services benchmarks (billable utilization,
  on-time delivery, engagement margin)

## Management frameworks in the product

- Balanced Scorecard (Kaplan–Norton) — perspectives model + portfolio balance read
- Critical/Key Success Factors (Rockart) — KSF catalog linking strategy to measures
- Hoshin Kanri — breakthrough vs daily-management dimensions
- 4 Disciplines of Execution — WIG vs sustaining dimensions
All descriptions are written in our own words; framework names are used nominatively.

## Provenance note

No text was copied verbatim from any source. Metric names are generic
industry vocabulary; descriptions and objective phrasing are original to
AIPOWER CO.,LTD. Default targets are round reference numbers a customer is
expected to tailor per cycle.
